# Klick Template Maker

A single-file photo-booth print template maker for Klick Photobooth. It runs entirely in the browser: nothing is uploaded, and the page works offline once loaded.

- Live page: https://lukea3671-claude.github.io/klick-template-maker/
- Offline copy: `Klick-Template-Maker.html` (save it, then open it in Chrome)

Paper sizes: 80 x 80 mm for 1 photo and 80 x 150 mm strips for 2, 3, 4 and 6 photos, rendered at 300 dpi. Seven designs, a black-and-white mode for thermal printers, see-through photo boxes on request, and an optional client logo at the top of the template. Only the photo boxes are pure black, so booth apps that detect black rectangles see the photo slots and nothing else.
